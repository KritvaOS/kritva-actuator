# Code of Conduct

This code applies to everyone who works on the Kritva Actuator project, including Kritvaos staff, interns, reviewers, contractors and visitors to the lab. It applies in the repository, in Issues and reviews, in meetings and chat, and in the laboratory.

## 1. Our commitments

We will:

- treat every team member with respect, regardless of background, role, experience or seniority;
- give and receive technical criticism professionally, about the work and not the person;
- credit other people's contributions honestly;
- ask questions openly; there is no penalty for not knowing something;
- record our assumptions, mistakes and open issues honestly, because hidden problems are the most dangerous ones in engineering;
- respect the independent reviewer's role, and never pressure a reviewer to pass work or to downgrade a finding.

## 2. Unacceptable behaviour

The following are not acceptable:

- harassment, discrimination, intimidation or personal attacks of any kind;
- dismissive or demeaning comments in reviews, meetings or chat;
- deliberately misreporting test results, falsifying evidence, or marking a requirement `PASS` without the required evidence;
- bypassing a safety rule or a gate, or encouraging someone else to do so;
- sharing proprietary material outside Kritvaos without authorisation.

## 3. Laboratory conduct

Safety is everyone's responsibility. In the lab:

- follow [requirements/SAFETY_REQUIREMENTS.md](requirements/SAFETY_REQUIREMENTS.md) and the test procedure in use;
- test only on the restrained fixture, and never resist the actuator by hand or attach it to a person;
- **anyone may stop a test at any time** for a safety concern, without needing to justify it first, and nobody will be criticised for doing so in good faith;
- report incidents and near misses on the same day; reports are used for learning, not blame;
- keep the bench tidy, and keep tools, cables and spare parts out of the clear zone.

## 4. Confidentiality

The repository and all related material are **proprietary and confidential** ([LICENSE](LICENSE)). Do not:

- publish, upload or share files, CAD, firmware, schematics, test data or screenshots outside approved Kritvaos systems;
- paste proprietary content into public tools or forums;
- reuse the material in other projects without written permission.

AI assistants used for review, such as the remote technical review described in [planning/v1/DECISIONS.md](planning/v1/DECISIONS.md) ACT-DEC-014, may only be used through accounts and workspaces approved by Kritvaos.

## 5. Reporting

Report concerns to the **Project Lead**. If the concern involves the Project Lead, report it to Kritvaos management. Reports are handled confidentially and promptly, and retaliation against anyone who reports in good faith is not tolerated.

## 6. Consequences

Depending on severity, breaches may lead to a private conversation, a formal warning, removal from the lab or the repository, or termination of the internship or contract, in line with Kritvaos policies.
